Playing in a minute

Download

Run SC-Client-Setup.exe, or unzip the portable version anywhere.

Sign in

Log in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft.

Press Play

Pick a version. The SC mod, your cosmetics and your settings are set up for you.

Questions

Is SC Client free?

Yes. The launcher, the Wardrobe and the chat are all free.

Is my account safe?

You sign in through Microsoft's own login page, the same way the official launcher does. SC Client never sees or stores your password.

Which Minecraft versions get cosmetics?

Fabric on 1.21.11 and 26.x. The launcher adds the SC mod to those versions by itself every time you press Play, and keeps it up to date.

Can other players see my cape?

Everyone using SC Client sees your hats, capes and wings. Players on other clients see your normal skin.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.

Is it open source?

Yes. The launcher and the SC mod are on GitHub under GPL-3.0.
